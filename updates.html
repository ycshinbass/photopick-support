What’s new

2.19.3 beta

In review on the Chrome Web Store

Submitted November 23, 2026 · Compared with 2.19.1 beta

Start from an album or search

Scan the photos in an open album and compare the results in the same tab. For a date range or your whole library, Photopick can open the Photos view in that tab and start when it’s ready. If you change scan settings, you can see the earlier and new settings side by side. A scan you can resume brings back its original dates and mode.

See what the scan is still doing

The panel keeps showing progress while Photopick compares photos it has already collected. You can see how long each stage took, how many files still need a closer look, and when a date range contains no photos. If a separate scan tab fails to load the list, Photopick tries again.

Come back to your results

You can hide the panel and let the scan run, stop it as you close the panel, or turn off automatic opening. When you return to the original list with scan history on, Photopick can bring back your results and previews. If a preview fails, retry it beside the photo. In crowded results, the previews on screen load first, and the review controls fit in shorter windows.

Switch to Accurate mode for a closer look

Fast mode shows the groups it could sort out and counts the photos that need Accurate mode. When you switch, Photopick checks its untouched suggestions again and keeps the choices you reviewed. It can use exact file sizes to choose between otherwise comparable originals. A HEIC or HEIF photo with unknown HDR needs another check before Photopick can suggest it for Trash.

Pick up where you left off

Photopick shows when it reuses a saved keep decision. Undo brings back your last review choices and draft. Review edits save in batches while you work, and you can also save them directly. If scan history runs out of room, Photopick clears older finished scans from the same account to make space.

Chrome permissions are the same as in 2.19.1 beta.

2.19.1 beta

Available on the Chrome Web Store

Availability confirmed November 23, 2026 · Compared with 2.17.6 beta

Let a scan run while you do something else

A scan can keep going while you switch tabs or minimize Chrome. If you turn on scan history, you can resume a stopped scan or reopen saved results with previews. During a comparison, the panel shows the capture date and how many files it has checked.

Get a suggestion when several shots look alike

Photopick can suggest one photo to keep by comparing previews on your device. When the top shots are close, it looks at details such as framing, face size and smiles. You’ll see when the recommendation is weak, and you can change which photos to keep.

Review a selection in a smaller window

You can filter results, change your keep choice in the comparison view and undo that choice. The review controls fit in shorter windows. If you open the selection list while a draft is saving, it opens when the save finishes. Photopick also checks the selection as Google Photos rearranges a crowded day or clears the checks after you cancel.

Start the same scan once

The Start scan button responds right away. Repeated clicks start one scan, and you’ll see a message if the same scan is running in another tab. You can switch between saved scans from the panel. Photopick also closes unused scan tabs when it finishes.

Chrome permissions

This version adds the Chrome alarms permission to help recover interrupted scans and close leftover scan tabs. The sites Photopick can access are the same.

Find copies and compare similar shots

The first beta could find potential duplicate photos and videos and group similar photos for side-by-side review. Fast mode used previews. Accurate mode could also check file details, including HDR when available. You chose what to keep and confirmed any move to Trash in Google Photos.
